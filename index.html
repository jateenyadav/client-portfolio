01 — About me

Campaigns built from zero

I'm a marketing executive in Dubai who runs campaigns end to end — strategy, copy, creative, launch, and the reporting that proves whether it worked.

For the past two years I've owned the full funnel at a founder-led pet care brand in the UAE: email marketing, social, SEO, content, influencer, paid, partnerships, and the promotional side of in-store activations and partner events. I've built things from zero rather than inherited. I ran a UAE-wide partner acquisition programme solo across English and Arabic-language channels, onboarding 34 partners, and built the tracking that showed which channels actually converted.

I write copy that sounds like a person wrote it, and I shift tone across audiences — Gen-Z consumer one day, B2B partner the next.
